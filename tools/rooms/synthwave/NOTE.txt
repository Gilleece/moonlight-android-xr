Woodford XR. First made for Depthray, Woodford XR's VR video player, and
borrowed from it for this app. Modelled in Blender and painted in vertex
colours, the ground, the sun and the vista all carrying their own, with a 4096
by 4096 atlas on the sofa.

Shipped in the app as the "Synthwave" environment.

The source, synthwave.glb (about 11 MB, the sofa's atlas embedded as a PNG), is
kept outside the repository. The mesh is the one the Synthwave_HighRes node
carries; the file's other nodes are markers and carry no geometry. The renderer
paints a part from its atlas or from its colours and never both, so the bake
splits that one primitive in two on its vertex colours: the 2,068 triangles
whose vertices are white take the atlas and the other 10,890 are painted from
their colours. Run from the top of the repository, with the glb and the
extracted PNG both outside it:

  python3 tools/bake_room.py --mesh Synthwave_HighRes \
      --texture synthwave_4k.png synthwave.glb \
      app/src/main/assets/rooms/synthwave.room

  python3 tools/atlas_astc.py synthwave_4k.png \
      --out app/src/main/assets/rooms/synthwave_0.atlas

The delivered atlas is already 4096, so it is encoded as it comes, each level a
Lanczos resize of it. The 2048 set is encoded from a 2048 Lanczos resize saved
as a PNG first, so its smaller levels are resized from that:

  python3 -c "from PIL import Image; Image.open('synthwave_4k.png').convert('RGB')\
.resize((2048, 2048), Image.LANCZOS).save('synthwave_2k.png', optimize=True)"
  python3 tools/atlas_astc.py synthwave_2k.png \
      --out app/src/main/assets/rooms/synthwave_lo_0.atlas

atlas_astc.py needs astcenc 5.x (ARM's astc-encoder, Apache-2.0) through
$ASTCENC or on the PATH. Both atlases are ASTC 6x6 at the default "thorough"
quality with their mip chains down to 1x1.

  synthwave.room           1,306,980 bytes, 26,169 vertices, 12,958 triangles,
                           two parts: the sofa, 4,874 vertices off the atlas,
                           and 21,295 painted from their colours
  synthwave_0.atlas        9,961,612 bytes, 4096 square, 13 levels
  synthwave_lo_0.atlas     2,497,784 bytes, 2048 square, 12 levels, the set
                           an XR2 Gen 1 headset reads

The glb's ScreenAnchor empty says the picture hangs at (0, 3.1, -14), 10 by
5.625 m, and its Viewer_Seated node is at (0, 1.2, 0). The room's row in
xr_room.c keeps the seat, raised 0.35 m and brought 0.10 m in, and hangs a
bigger picture than the anchor: 14 by 7.875 m at (0, 4.5, -14), which keeps its
bottom edge half a metre off the ground. There are no walls, so the row puts
them 1800 m out where nothing can reach them, marks the room open so nothing
clamps the picture, and the far plane follows the geometry's own reach, the
sky 1,800 m out. The picture may be resized, the glow starts on, and the room
is drawn at 0.53 of its baked brightness.

rooms/thumbs/synthwave.jpg, the picker tile, is a 256 square centre crop of the
owner's own picture of the room.
