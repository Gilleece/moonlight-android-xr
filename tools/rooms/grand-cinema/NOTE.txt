Woodford XR. First made for Depthray, Woodford XR's VR video player, and
borrowed from it for this app. Modelled in Blender with the lighting baked into
one 8192 by 8192 atlas, so the room ships unlit and needs nothing at runtime but
its texture.

Shipped in the app as the "Grand Cinema" environment.

The source, grand_cinema.glb (about 12.5 MB, the atlas embedded as a PNG), is
kept outside the repository. The mesh is the one the Grand-Cinema-HighRes_Baked
node carries, one mesh and one material; the file's other nodes are markers and
carry no geometry. Run from the top of the repository, with the glb and the
extracted PNG both outside it:

  python3 tools/bake_room.py --mesh Grand-Cinema-HighRes_Baked \
      --texture grand_cinema_8k.png grand_cinema.glb \
      app/src/main/assets/rooms/grand_cinema.room

  python3 tools/atlas_astc.py grand_cinema_8k.png --size 4096 \
      --out app/src/main/assets/rooms/grand_cinema_0.atlas
  python3 tools/atlas_astc.py grand_cinema_8k.png --size 2048 \
      --out app/src/main/assets/rooms/grand_cinema_lo_0.atlas

atlas_astc.py needs astcenc 5.x (ARM's astc-encoder, Apache-2.0) through
$ASTCENC or on the PATH. Both atlases are ASTC 6x6 at the default "thorough"
quality with their mip chains down to 1x1, each level a Lanczos resize of the
8192 source.

  grand_cinema.room        2,134,996 bytes, 43,670 vertices, 17,790 triangles,
                           one part painted from the atlas
  grand_cinema_0.atlas     9,961,612 bytes, 4096 square, 13 levels
  grand_cinema_lo_0.atlas  2,497,784 bytes, 2048 square, 12 levels, the set
                           an XR2 Gen 1 headset reads

The glb's ScreenAnchor empty is where the picture hangs and how big it can be:
its origin, (0, 7.4, -15.42), is the screen's centre and its scale, 22 by
12.375, the largest width and height in metres. Its Viewer_Seated node is at
(0, 4.8, 6.0), partway up the rake. Neither carries geometry, so the bake leaves
them out and the numbers live in the room's row in xr_room.c instead: the seat
raised 0.40 m, the picture filling the whole anchor and not resizable, the glow
off to start with, and the atlas drawn at 0.21 of its baked brightness.

rooms/thumbs/grand_cinema.jpg, the picker tile, is a 256 square centre crop of
the owner's own picture of the room.
