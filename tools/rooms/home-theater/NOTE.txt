"Home Theater" is the owner's own model. Copyright (c) 2026 Sean Gilleece.
Modelled in Blender with the lighting baked into one 8192 by 8192 atlas, so the
room ships unlit and needs nothing at runtime but its texture.

Shipped in the app as the "Home Theater" environment.

The source, home_theater.glb (about 38 MB, the atlas embedded as a PNG), is
kept outside the repository. The mesh is the one the
Home-Theater-HighRes_Baked node carries; the file's other nodes are markers
and carry no geometry. Run from the top of the repository, with the glb and
the extracted PNG both outside it:

  python3 tools/bake_room.py --mesh Home-Theater-HighRes_Baked \
      --texture home_theater_8k.png home_theater.glb \
      app/src/main/assets/rooms/home_theater.room

  python3 tools/atlas_astc.py home_theater_8k.png --size 4096 \
      --out app/src/main/assets/rooms/home_theater_0.atlas
  python3 tools/atlas_astc.py home_theater_8k.png --size 2048 \
      --out app/src/main/assets/rooms/home_theater_lo_0.atlas

atlas_astc.py needs astcenc 5.x (ARM's astc-encoder, Apache-2.0) through
$ASTCENC or on the PATH. Both atlases are ASTC 6x6 at the default "thorough"
quality with their mip chains down to 1x1, each level a Lanczos resize of the
8192 source.

  home_theater.room        573,540 bytes, 16,821 vertices, 5,876 triangles
  home_theater_0.atlas   9,961,612 bytes, 4096 square, 13 levels
  home_theater_lo_0.atlas 2,497,784 bytes, 2048 square, 12 levels, the set
                          an XR2 Gen 1 headset reads

The glb's ScreenAnchor empty is where the picture hangs and how big it can be:
its origin, (0, 1.55, -3.132), is the screen's centre and its scale, 3.6 by
2.025, the largest width and height in metres. Its Viewer_Seated node is at
(0, 1.15, 1.12). Neither carries geometry, so the bake leaves them out and the
numbers live in homeTheaterParams in xr_room.c instead.

rooms/thumbs/home_theater.jpg, the picker tile, is a 256 square centre crop of
the owner's own picture of the room.
