Woodford XR. Made in code by tools/make_controller.py: a generic VR controller,
a handle, a head with a thumbstick, a trigger and a ring over the top, painted
in vertex colours, a neutral dark grey with a lighter ring, and no texture.

Shipped in the app as models/controller.room and drawn at each hand's grip
when "Show the controllers" is on.

Run from the top of the repository, with the glb outside it:

  python3 tools/make_controller.py controller.glb
  python3 tools/bake_room.py controller.glb \
      app/src/main/assets/models/controller.room

The glb is only a step between the two and is not kept; the script makes the
same one every time.

  controller.room   58,300 bytes, 890 vertices, 1,592 triangles, one part
                    painted from its vertex colours, no atlas

The model is a right hand controller in OpenXR's grip space, in metres: the
origin is the middle of the handle where the palm closes round it, +x points to
the right, +y up and -z forward along a straightened index finger. The renderer
places it at the grip pose as it is and mirrors it in x for the left hand.
